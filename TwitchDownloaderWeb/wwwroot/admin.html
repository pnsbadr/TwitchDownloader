Teams

    Users

      Links

      A team registration link lets one person make an account (or use theirs) and a new team they own. An owner link (from Teams) makes whoever opens it the owner of an existing team. Each works once and expires after 7 days.

        Moderators

        Moderators help run the app. Only you (the Owner) can invite them and pick what they can do. Like team members, they start with no permissions. An invite link works once and expires after 7 days.

          Twitch

          Lets the app hear from Twitch the moment a team's channel starts or stops streaming, so VOD cards and players update on every open page without a refresh. Twitch only sends this to a registered Twitch app signed in with a Twitch account (any account; it needs no permissions and never sees your password). One account can watch 5 channels; others keep working as before.

          Make one at dev.twitch.tv: any name, OAuth Redirect URL http://localhost, Client Type Public.

            Reports

            Bugs, errors and crashes: the server's errors, background jobs that failed, pages that crashed, and problems people reported from the account menu. Each one says who it happened to, when, where, and what was going on.
