This is the app's staff account for running the app (teams, links, moderators). It's separate from team accounts: to work in a team's library, make a normal account with a team link.

Open this page on the computer running the app, as http://localhost:5254/admin.html.

    Team links

    A team registration link lets one person make an account (or use theirs) and a new team they own. An owner link makes whoever opens it the owner of an existing team. Each works once and expires after 7 days.
