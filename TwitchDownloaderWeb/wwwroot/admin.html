      Links

      A team registration link lets one person make an account (or use theirs) and a new team they own. An owner link (from Teams) makes whoever opens it the owner of an existing team. Each works once and expires after 7 days.

        Moderators

        Moderators help run the app. Only you (the Owner) can invite them and pick what they can do. Like team members, they start with no permissions. An invite link works once and expires after 7 days.
